The design

Why it looks and feels the way it does

We started from the two things people said most often about other mouth tapes: it is unpleasant to take off, and it does not stay on. Every choice below comes from those two complaints.

01

Silicone gel, not hot-melt glue

Many mouth tapes use hot-melt adhesive. We chose a 0.3 mm layer of silicone gel, which costs more. It rests against the skin instead of gripping it, so it peels away in the morning without tugging and leaves no sticky residue.

02

A shape that follows your mouth

The butterfly outline follows the muscles around your lips, so the tape moves with your face and stays in place through the night. The wings are cut to stay clear of the corners of your mouth.

03

A small slit in the center

The opening in the middle makes the tape feel less restrictive, especially if you are new to mouth tape.

04

Soft, matte, lavender

The face layer is a nonwoven made with milk protein fiber, in matte lavender with a small gold crescent moon. It looks like part of a night routine, not a bandage.

Three steps

How to use

  1. 1

    Confirm you can breathe comfortably through your nose.

  2. 2

    Before sleep, apply one tape horizontally across clean, dry, closed lips and press gently.

  3. 3

    In the morning, peel off slowly, starting from one end.

Before you use it

Important safety information

  • For adults 18 and over only.
  • Allergen notice: contains milk protein fiber. Do not use if you have a milk or dairy-related allergy.
  • Do not use if you have nasal congestion, difficulty breathing through your nose, or a respiratory condition.
  • Do not use after consuming alcohol, sedatives, or sleep medication.
  • Stop using it immediately if skin irritation occurs.
  • HushLip is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or medical condition.
